Nauto is a predictive AI platform for commercial fleet safety. Using in-vehicle AI dash cams and computer vision, it monitors driver behavior in real time, delivers predictive collision alerts before incidents occur, scores driver risk with its VERA system, and powers driver coaching, exoneration, and incident intelligence. Nauto also supports DVIR, IFTA, and ELD fleet-compliance workflows across trucking, delivery, construction, passenger transit, and field-service fleets. Backed by Greylock, Playground Global, and SoftBank Vision Fund, Nauto is SOC 2 Type 1/2 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified.
